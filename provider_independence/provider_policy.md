# Provider Independence Policy

External AI providers can support INVENTHOR, but INVENTHOR must not fully depend on them.

Provider = support engine.
INVENTHOR Brain = shared system intelligence.
Local fallback = minimum survival intelligence.

Research evidence independence (source-only; not active in installed Main): see `research_evidence_independence.md`. The implementation belongs to the federated `inventhor-knowledge-filter` repository; no duplicate Brain engine is authorized.
